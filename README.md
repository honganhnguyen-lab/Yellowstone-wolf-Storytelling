## Project Title: A Howling Success: The Wolves that saved Yellowstone

## Overview
Our team is telling the story of wolves in Yellowstone from their extinction in the 1920s to their reintroduction decades later. Their extinction caused elk, deer, and other species to overpopulate, which severely damaged vegetation and disrupted the park’s entire ecosystem. After a few decades, researchers proposed reintroducing wolves to restore the park’s balance, and the impact was felt in both the vegetation and ecosystem. The repopulation led to vegetation rebounding, animal populations rebalancing, and the beginning of a healed ecosystem. We chose this story because it was both inspiring and cautionary. The removal of one species, the wolf, led to a catastrophic cascading impact in many ecological areas. This point was powerful in displaying how one player in a very large intricate puzzle that is nature can make the whole thing crumble. This tale was cautionary because it was a prime example of humans overstepping their bounds and hurting the environment. Overall this story was chosen because it was about how a feared species became an unknown hero and a reminder of the responsibility we have to protect our planet.
